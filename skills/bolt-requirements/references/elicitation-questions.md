# Elicitation questions

Ask in order, in business language. Do not move on while a group has an unanswered trap word.

## Who

- Who uses this, by job title? What does each start and finish?
- What should one person see that another cannot?
- Is anyone outside the company involved? Do they have accounts, or reach in from chat?
- Who may override a decision, and is that recorded?

## What

- What things does this manage, in the business's names?
- For each, which facts are not derivable from others?
- What distinguishes two things that look the same?
- Which facts change over time, and must past values be provable?
- Which things belong to other things?

## Transitions

- What states does each thing live through?
- What moves it, who, on what trigger?
- What is forbidden?
- Where must a human review before something happens?
- What happens to a rejected record?

## Numbers

- Every amount: what is it, in what unit, who decides it?
- Which numbers freeze once decided?
- Which are derived, and what is the derived number called?
- What rounding, currencies, tax modes and decimals apply, per jurisdiction?
- Which are guesses or targets rather than facts?

## Triggers

- What work runs on a schedule? Who knows it is due?
- What work follows an event?
- What is automatic, and what waits for a person?
- When automated work fails, who notices, and what does failed mean for the records?

## The outside world

- Which systems hold this data? Which owns each fact?
- Which third parties take part?
- What must be produced for them, in what format, by when?
- Which obligations are legal or regulatory, and what is the evidence?
- What may wait when an external system is down?

## Outputs

- What documents, files and exports result? Who receives each, what is on it, what format, does it need a signature?
- What reports, with which numbers, for whom?
- What messages go out, and who should not see them?
- What must be provable later?

## Closing

- If only one were built, which saves the most pain?
- What would you do on the first Monday with it?
- What is the worst thing it could get wrong?
