# Elicitation questions

Ask only questions that resolve consequential gaps after reading supplied context and workspace source:

- **Who:** job titles, what each sees/changes, external accounts vs chat, overrides & audit.
- **What:** business entities, non-derivable facts, what distinguishes look-alikes, history/effective-dated facts, ownership.
- **Transitions:** states, triggers, who moves each, forbidden transitions, human approvals, rejection handling.
- **Numbers:** amounts/units/owners, frozen vs derived figures, rounding/currencies/tax/jurisdiction rules, targets vs facts.
- **Triggers:** schedules, events, automatic vs manual steps, failure handling and visibility.
- **Outside world & outputs:** owning systems, third-party handoffs/formats/deadlines, legal obligations, outage tolerance, documents/reports/messages/proofs.
- **Priority:** highest-value first slice, day-1 workflow, worst possible mistake.
